A make.fun bounty entry: one line of text, 1–240 characters, giving the
judge a quiet, earned moment of pride for someone in a scene.

## The line

> After three years of saving, she slid the key into her own front door
> and turned it twice, just to hear it twice.

Three years of saving lands in one small, physical act — turning the key
twice just to hear it twice. The pride is earned inside the line and
arrives with the last words.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`, checking first that it
is 1–240 characters of non-blank UTF-8 text, as the bounty gate requires.

Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
